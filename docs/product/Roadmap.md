# Development Philosophy

XEEO follows a documentation-first, architecture-driven development process.

The workflow is:

```text
Plan

↓

Design

↓

Review

↓

Build

↓

Test

↓

Deploy

↓

Iterate
```

Every major architectural decision is documented before implementation, while feature development follows an iterative approach to deliver a stable and maintainable platform.
